About Me

Hey! My name is Jacob, and I'm an Information Technology student who has a huge passion for skateboarding. For me, skateboarding is more than just riding a board. It's about freedom, creativity, and enjoying every moment on four wheels. Whether I'm practicing tricks, cruising around, or simply spending time on my skateboard, skating always brings me happiness. Through this blog, I want to share my experiences, my skateboard setup, and the reasons why I love skating.

My Skateboarding Journal

1. Why I Love Skateboarding

Date: October 16, 2026

Skateboarding has always been something that makes me feel free and happy. There's something special about stepping on a skateboard, feeling the wheels roll, and forgetting about everything else for a while. What I love most about skating is that there's always something new to learn. Every trick takes patience, practice, and determination. Even when I fall or fail a trick, I always find a reason to get back up and try again.

2. My Skateboard Setup

Date: October 12, 2026

One of the most exciting parts of skateboarding is building a skateboard that matches your personal preferences. My current skateboard setup consists of a Amoire Libre 8.5-inch deck, Lurpiv Solids trucks, Spitfire Formula Four wheels, and Bronson bearings. I chose these components because I wanted a skateboard that feels comfortable and enjoyable to ride. Having my own skateboard setup makes every skating session even more special.

3. Lessons I've Learned From Skating

Date: October 8, 2026

Skateboarding has taught me many valuable lessons that I can apply to my everyday life. One of the biggest lessons I've learned is that progress takes time. You can't expect to land every trick on your first try. Sometimes it takes dozens or even hundreds of attempts. Skating has also taught me to be patient, stay determined, and never give up easily. Every small improvement reminds me that hard work and consistency can lead to something rewarding.
