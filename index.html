About Me

Hey! My name is Jacob, and I'm an Information Technology student who has a huge passion for skateboarding. For me, skateboarding is more than just riding a board. It's about freedom, creativity, and enjoying every moment on four wheels. Whether I'm practicing tricks, cruising around, or simply spending time on my skateboard, skating always brings me happiness. Through this blog, I want to share my experiences, my skateboard setup, and the reasons why I love skating.

My Skateboarding Journal

Why I Love Skateboarding

October 16, 2026

Skateboarding has always been something that makes me feel free and happy. There's something special about stepping on a board, feeling the wheels roll, and forgetting about everything else. Even when I fall or fail a trick, I always find a reason to get back up and try again.

My Skateboard Setup

October 12, 2026

One of the most exciting parts of skateboarding is building a board that matches your preferences. My setup consists of a CCS Amoire Libre 8.5-inch deck, Lurpiv Solids trucks, Spitfire Formula Four wheels, and Bronson bearings. Having my own setup makes every skating session even more special.

Lessons From Skating

October 8, 2026

Skateboarding has taught me many valuable lessons that I apply to my life. One of the biggest lessons is that progress takes time. Sometimes it takes dozens or hundreds of attempts before landing a trick. Skating has taught me patience, determination, and consistency.
